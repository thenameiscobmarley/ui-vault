From farm to cup

Six weeks, four hands.

  1. Week 1

    Picked ripe

    Only the red cherries, by hand, on the slopes of Huila at 1,800 m.

  2. Week 2

    Washed & dried

    Fermented 36 hours, then sun-dried on raised beds for twenty days.

  3. Week 5

    Roasted light

    Twelve-kilo batches, dropped just after first crack. Every Tuesday.

  4. Week 6

    In your cup

    Rested seven days, then posted the same afternoon.
