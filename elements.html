Element designs · mix & match

270+ elements, your mix.

Every element comes in many designs. There are pearlescent, chrome, deep-blue-metal and brutal buttons, glass and ticket cards, iOS and day/night switches, and plenty more. Pick one design per element, or start from a kit. Your mix applies to this whole site, and you can try it on the test page. Every design works with every one of the 87 themes.

§ Kits

Start from a kit.

A kit is a theme plus one design for every element. Apply one, then swap any single element below. It's still your mix.

§ Elements

Every element, many ways.

Click Use to apply a design site-wide (click again to remove it). Code shows the HTML, the raw CSS, and the closest Python and C++ equivalents.

Use them in your own pages: load lib/css/parts.css (everything) or just lib/css/parts/btn.css, then put data-uv-btn="pearl" on <html> or on any element. Colours come from the active theme's tokens.
