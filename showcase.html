    Is it really free?
    Yes — MIT licensed. Use it in anything, commercial included.
    Do I need React?
    No. It's plain CSS + vanilla JS and works with any framework or none.
    Will it lag on old laptops?
    Effects use transforms and opacity, pause offscreen and respect reduced-motion.
    Can I theme it?
    Set --uv-accent and --uv-accent-2. That's it.
