§ 01 — Quick start
Running in three steps.
Link the hosted files, or unzip the download next to your HTML and use relative paths.

    

      
1
Add the two files
Put the CSS in <head> and the script anywhere with defer.

        

      
2
Write HTML with uv-* classes
Components are classes. Behaviours are data-uv attributes. Themes are data-uv-theme.

        

      
3
Call the JS API when you need it
Everything lives on window.UV, and every function works on its own.

§ 05 — Effects
Stack them. Break things.
CSS effects are uv-fx-* classes; JavaScript effects are data-uv-fx="…". Hover, click and move your mouse over the tiles. Click a label to copy its usage.
